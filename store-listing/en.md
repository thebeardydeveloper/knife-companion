## Full Description (4000 chars max)
KnifeCompanion is the ultimate companion app for knife makers and steel enthusiasts.

**Steel Encyclopedia**
Explore a comprehensive reference for 50+ blade steels. Every entry includes:
• Full chemical composition (C, Cr, Mo, V, W, Co, and more)
• Mechanical properties: toughness, edge retention, corrosion resistance, sharpenability
• Hardness range (HRC)
• Step-by-step heat treatment guide with temperatures, quench media, and temper cycles
• Origin and characteristics

**Community Gallery**
Share your knife making work with a community that understands the craft:
• Post photos of your builds with full knife metadata (steel, blade length, handle materials)
• Like and comment on other makers' work
• Explore a feed of knives from makers around the world
• Tap any maker to view their profile and portfolio

**Offline First**
The steel encyclopedia is fully available offline. Reference data is cached locally so you always have it when you need it — at the forge, on the road, or anywhere without a connection.

**Bilingual**
Full support for English and Spanish. Switch languages instantly from Settings.

---
Whether you're a seasoned bladesmith or just starting out, KnifeCompanion puts decades of metallurgical knowledge in your pocket.

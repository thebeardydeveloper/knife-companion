## Full Description (4000 chars max)
KnifeCompanion is the ultimate companion app for knife makers and blade steel enthusiasts. Whether you're a seasoned bladesmith or just starting out, it puts decades of metallurgical knowledge in your pocket — and connects you with a community that understands the craft.

**Steel Encyclopedia**
Explore a comprehensive reference for 50+ blade steels. Every entry includes:
• Full chemical composition (C, Cr, Mo, V, W, Co, and more) — displayed as a periodic table grid with element categories
• Mechanical properties: toughness, edge retention, corrosion resistance, and sharpenability
• Hardness range (HRC)
• Step-by-step heat treatment guide with temperatures, quench media, and temper cycles
• Origin and historical characteristics

**Craftsmen**
Connect with the knife making community:
• Browse the latest work from makers around the world
• Share your own builds with full metadata: steel used, blade length, handle materials, and notes
• Like and comment on other makers' posts
• Search for craftsmen by username and explore their portfolio

**Suppliers** *(coming soon)*
Find where to source your materials — search by supplier name or by material type.

**Tutorials** *(coming soon)*
Step-by-step guides covering forging, heat treatment, handle work, and finishing.

**Offline First**
The steel encyclopedia is fully cached locally. Reference data is always available — at the forge, on the road, or anywhere without a connection.

**Bilingual**
Full support for English and Spanish. Switch languages instantly from Settings.
